# Stakeholders and trust concerns

These are role-based research hypotheses, not interview findings. They must be
validated with actual stakeholders before the design is used operationally.

| Stakeholder | Goals | Trust concerns | Likely tension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logistics operator | Triage quickly and understand next steps | Clear reasons, low false-negative risk, practical recommendations | Speed vs. review burden |
| Logistics manager | Coordinate teams and service recovery | Consistent prioritisation, accountable approvals, useful audit trail | Throughput vs. escalation |
| Compliance officer | Interpret customs, safety, and regulatory evidence | Current authoritative sources, provenance, explicit unknowns | Coverage vs. unsupported interpretation |
| Regulator | Inspect how a recommendation was produced | Reconstructable decisions, traceable evidence, accountable owners | Audit detail vs. privacy/data minimisation |
| Customer | Receive accurate, timely updates and protect personal data | No fabricated status or disclosure; understandable communication | Personalisation vs. privacy |
| Data Protection Officer | Reduce personal-data exposure | Redaction, purpose limitation, retention controls | Traceable records vs. minimised retention |
| AI engineer | Monitor model limits and failures | Reproducible data, metrics, drift/error visibility | Model utility vs. complexity |
| Software architect | Preserve bounded, testable components | Explicit contracts, failure containment, trace links | Modularity vs. latency/operational cost |

The system drafts and routes recommendations for staff; it does not dispatch
vehicles, release goods, or make other autonomous logistics decisions.
